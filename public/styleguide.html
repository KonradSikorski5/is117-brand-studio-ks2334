Agreed brand direction

Audience

Students who work

They go from class straight to a retail shift and are on their feet about 8–10 hours.

Archetype

Everyman

Down to earth and relatable. No hype.

Visual direction

Postmodernist

Bold, blocky type and loud color. Chosen because it "looked like a site I would buy from."

06 / Voice

Chill,
straight-up.

Voice traits: chill, straight-up, encouraging.

Example line

"Long day ahead? This one's got you."

Talks like a coworker, not a salesperson.

Honest persuasion on the storefront

Unity: "Made for students who work." It helps visitors see quickly if the shirt is meant for people like them. It is true because this is the audience we chose.

Commitment: an "Is this your day?" check (class first, then a shift; on your feet most of it) comes before the sizes. If it matches, picking a size is the next small step. Nothing is fake: no reviews, countdowns, or stock numbers.
