Konrad Sikorski · IS117-005 · Sites checked Nov 3, 2026

**Idea:** One everyday white tee for students who commute to campus.

## Three sites I compared

I shopped each site the same way: US desktop, one white men's crewneck, size M, regular shipping. A few carts ended up with an L or XL, but every size costs the same. Screenshots of each product page and cart are in the `screenshots` folder of this repo.

## Two positions

1. **Simple everyday buy:** One white tee. Clear fit. One honest price.
2. **Made to last:** A white tee with real fabric facts for students who want it to last.

## Two persona hypotheses (not tested yet)

- **Persona A — commuting student:** Buys between class and work. Needs the size, the price, and a fast checkout. Worries about the wrong fit and surprise shipping costs.
- **Persona B — budget student who wants it to last:** Needs fabric and care facts. Worries about paying for a shirt that shrinks or wears out fast.

## My choice: Persona A

For a commuting student buying one everyday tee, my store helps with picking the right size and knowing the full price before checkout. I emphasize a size guide next to the sizes and a total with shipping. I still need to check that students care more about fit and total price than fabric details.

I picked A because the biggest problem I saw was unclear cost. ASKET's add-to-cart panel showed no shipping, and Uniqlo's cart listed it as TBD. Only Buck Mason showed an estimate early.

## Brand

- **Archetype:** Everyperson
- **Voice:** plain, friendly, honest
- **Look:** Modernist-inspired. I'm using the idea of a strong grid and clear order from Josef Müller-Brockmann's *Musica Viva* poster (1957, MoMA), so size and price are easy to find. I'll reuse my [Brand Studio](https://github.com/KonradSikorski5/is117-brand-studio-ks2334) style guide.

## Honest reasons to act

| Element | Principle | Why it could help | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free size guide with real measurements before the Add to Cart button | Reciprocity | Useful help first builds trust | It doesn't prove more sales |
| Plain, friendly student voice | Liking | Feels like it's from someone like them | I can't measure this yet |

I won't use fake reviews, fake countdowns, or fake "low stock" messages.

## Testable promise

- **User story:** As a commuting student unsure about fit, I want measurements next to the sizes so I can pick the right size.
- **Feature:** Size guide beside the size buttons, plus a total with shipping before checkout.
- **Check:** A classmate finds the size guide and the total without help.

## Assumptions

- Personas A and B are guesses based on three websites, not real customers.
- Three sites and one classmate don't prove demand.
- Prices and pages may change after Nov 3, 2026.

**Open question:** Do students care more about fit and total price, or about fabric quality?
